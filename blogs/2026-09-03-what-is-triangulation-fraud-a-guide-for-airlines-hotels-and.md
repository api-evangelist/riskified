Riskified’s most recent analysis of the travel fraud landscape underscores its evolution beyond stolen-card transactions. One threat that has emerged over the past few years is triangulation fraud, a scheme that can leave travelers with a legitimate-looking reservation while shifting the financial risk to the airline, hotel or other travel provider. It’s just one of the topics Riskified experts explored during the latest Risk Rundown: Travel webinar .
